# YouTube rules we follow (checked by /script, /edit, /publish)

Goal: the channel stays eligible for the YouTube Partner Program and never gets a strike.

## Monetization (YPP) — "inauthentic content" + "reused content" policies
- Every video is ORIGINAL: our own script, our own narration (the owner on camera), our own edit.
  Never translate or re-shoot another creator's Short beat-for-beat. References give format and topic ideas only.
- Not mass-produced: no templated, interchangeable videos. Each script has a specific hook, specific facts, a point of view.
- Added value is visible: commentary, explanation, examples, a takeaway for the viewer.
- AI b-roll is illustration behind a real human — fine. A video that is only AI footage + TTS is not what we make.

## Disclosure
- At upload tick "Altered or synthetic content" when AI-generated backgrounds are used (compose's .md says so).
- Never present AI footage as real events, real people or real places in a misleading way.

## Community guidelines (hard stops)
- No medical, nutrition or financial advice presented as certain: cite a credible source in the description and
  add "ఇది సాధారణ సమాచారం మాత్రమే — డాక్టర్/నిపుణుడిని సంప్రదించండి" when the topic touches health, medicine, diet or money.
- No harassment, no mocking real identifiable people, no hate, no dangerous challenges, no sexual content,
  nothing that targets children, no graphic violence, no misinformation on elections/health.
- "Funny / annoying / silly India moments" are about situations and habits, never about a named person, caste,
  religion, region or gender as a group.
- Copyright: no third-party clips, no copyrighted music. Music only from `assets/music/` (owner-licensed) or none.
- Thumbnails/titles must match the content (no clickbait that the video does not deliver).

## Metadata hygiene
- Title ≤ 100 chars, says the topic honestly; description with sources; 8-12 relevant hashtags (#Shorts included);
  no hashtag stuffing, no misleading tags.
- Audio: -14 LUFS, no clipping. Video: 1080x1920, ≤ 60 s preferred, hard max 180 s.
